OET Writing Coach

WRITING · REFERRAL, DISCHARGE & OTHER LETTERS

Paste a practice letter (or case notes) and get criterion-level feedback on the six OET writing criteria:

  • Purpose — is your main request clear immediately?
  • Content — the right case notes selected, nothing invented or missing
  • Conciseness & Clarity — word-count targeting (180–200 words), trimming filler
  • Genre & Style — the register and conventions examiners expect
  • Organisation & Layout — paragraph logic and ordering
  • Language — grammar, vocabulary, recurring error patterns

Write → feedback → fix → repeat. The point is not a magic score — it is knowing exactly what to practise next.
